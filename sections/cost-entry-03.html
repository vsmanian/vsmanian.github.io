Tell me something true about yourself

May 2026

I am waiting for you to flinch. Tell me this was all in my head - that I read the room wrong, or that I was in the wrong room in the first place. That you cannot see through the intensity that's coming towards you, layered in caution and deniability and one too many deflections.

I am waiting for you to tell me that there's a version where you see everything I see but feel nothing that I feel. That you don't sense the dip in my voice, that little change in inflection when I say a word, for it to mean anything other than the word itself. There's no subtext, no undercurrent, no foreshadowing that my life is just waiting for the drop of that last feather to topple it in its entirety. That you are not the cause and end of it.

I am waiting for you to flinch and tell me this isn't true. But I don't want you to.
