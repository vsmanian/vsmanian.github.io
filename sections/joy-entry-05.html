What I Want for You and Me This New Year

January 2026

The kind of love that says "Let me hold your bag while you warm your hands" on a cold winter day
To be permeable to wonder*
The strength to go where you are cherished

To invite respect and curiosity for what you know,
patience and grace for what you don't
Permission to dream audaciously and to let yourself be pulled towards it like gravity

To remember, always,
that it is a big wide world,
and that even when you stand by yourself,
you are never alone

To employ your money in ways
that allow you to be generous
to those down on their luck

Ask for help and give it
As naturally as you breathe

To never forget to be whimsical
and laugh often

To be true to yourself,
and to be kind always

* my favourite phrase from the book Martyr! by Kaveh Akbar
