Arrival

February 2026

I've come to love reading on the train. It was an acquired taste for a woman, the indulgence in one's imagination, the temerity to get lost in another world in a public place. I believe that everything has its time - even books. The fact that I am reading Arundhati Roy's memoir at 32, the exact age when she wrote her first public piece, is no coincidence. Rebellious daughters of towering mothers find their way into the secret sisterhood. Reading on train makes me believe I could do anything I wanted to and that I would reach my destination, both in matter and mind. I have different books for different routes, to reflect their varied objectives. I put my head down until I reach where I am heading, not unlike life. But when the January sun seeps through the skyscrapers, through the large window that I love on trains, I can't help myself but close my eyes and let it take me.
