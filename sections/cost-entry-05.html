Is this what you wanted?

June 2026

I hate that you turned my imperfect chords into something beautiful - you said you'd teach me how to and never did

That you touched my fingers in the same breath as telling me you were spoken for - and sought refuge in a pencil as if the 3 inches would keep you safe

That I said "It must be so boring to watch me play" and was met with a silence that knew I lied knowing the truth

That you as a concept was not supposed to exist. But you do and now the soma is wearing off and I have to survive it alone

That I knew you'd never give a straight answer - I have a cat, I know how this works - and still couldn't stop myself from asking a straight question

That you denied having lasting feelings while giving it the home I love the most, the one that brought me to you

That I did not pick up the phone that morning, and that I'll never know what you would have said when you didn't have time to think

That what started with you holding the door open for me to leave, slowly became me having to make the choice to leave while you stood nearby - and you did that even in the end

That you pushed it all under the armour built to protect the boy from Grimsby. That he would have given anything to be loved the way you are now

And yet, you are so painfully stupid that specific way smart people are, and the only thing you need is for you to see yourself, to allow yourself to

I hate that now I know your friend Tim and that's who I fucking get to keep instead of you
