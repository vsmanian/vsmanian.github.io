Beef Season 2 Episode 1

April 2026

I had to wait to come back across the Atlantic to finally catch up. And as someone who holds the proud badge of "Not binging" against my will, I watched the first episode today.

Oscar Isaac and Carey Mulligan reunite. It wasn't that long ago that I watched 'Inside Llewyn Davis' and they carry the same dynamics - her being holier-than-thou but not as accomplished (+ a British accent in Beef), him talented but insufferable. The fact that this season is about 4 people instead of 2 is something my jury is still out on. Beef season 1 had a lightness about it, the levity that comes from Steven Yuen feeling alive when Ali Wong chases him. The scene with Young Mazino and Ali Wong jumping on the bed after weeks of catfishing was poetry. It might be harsh to judge one episode against an entire season but so far, the lack of whimsy is a downgrade (The only exception is the scene where Oscar Isaac has his golf club raised and the naive-in-love couple stare for a second before running away) The smarts and imagery of season 1 still exists though (the almost road crash was a nice little callback without being on the nose)

One of the surprises was calling Mulligan / Crane's aesthetic sense as colonial. It does a lot - South Korea's own history with colonialism from Japan, and a commentary on how "traditional" aesthetics are viewed in a modern, multi-cultural world. It could sprawl into its own theme in the season. But there's too much already at play: commentaries and consequences of late-stage capitalism, complexities of old relationship, fragility of new ones (and the painful reminder of the US healthcare system) and 4 main characters. Is the season biting more than it can chew? Just have to wait and find out.

PS: Oscar Isaac is delightful as always, even when he is an a-hole.
