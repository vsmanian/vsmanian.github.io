IT'S NOT GOOD ENOUGH

August 2026

What do you want from me? Because I don't want whatever this half-assed thing you are offering me is. Fuck your deleted paragraphs, your vibe-coded pages, and your Instagram reposts. It infuriates me and it is insulting to us both.

You made my 2026 list come true, and it's only August. So here's an updated list of what I want for you and me now.

I want you. I want your stupid face and your skinny thighs that are near impossible to look away from. The chest that has too much hair and not enough me and I want to fix that ridiculous situation.

I want to play music with you into the night till our fingers bleed. I want those fingers inside me every time I dare say your name. Your neighbours should be grateful you soundproofed the living room.

I want to quibble over random threads, from Japanese oners to Jeremy, feeding our endless curiosity for the world. Matched only by our endless curiosity for each other.

I want to be demanding and cold and bossy and have it all shatter the moment I look into your eyes. I want to use your own words against you in arguments, yet find you shut me up in a way I don't see coming. Neither will know who had the last word as we settle the score in bed. We will be teenagers all over again.

I want to yap sitting on the kitchen counter while you make me breakfast. I will kiss you and make you laugh and let that nourish me instead, while the food chars.

I want to fix your console table and have sex on it till we have to fix it again. We will fix it to break it, again and again, till we have no choice but to throw the damn thing out. See if the furniture aisle at Ikea has anything that passes our test.

I want to sing terrible songs I like terribly while you roll your eyes and learn to like it too. How else will you show remorse when you inevitably screw up?

I will correct your code and you will correct my chords and we will push each other to be more than ourselves. We will study each other to understand ourselves.

Even on days that are quiet, we won't be. I want to lie on the couch reading while you tinker around on your guitar, my legs in your way but you wouldn't want it any other way. I want the TV shows we watch for the tenth time and still find something new to delight in, debate in. Any time away will be agony and any time together will feed our addiction that is the other.

We will edge each other till we learn what infinity truly is. We will have each other till there are no condoms left in the 2 mile radius, leaving nail marks and bite marks like stripes on our shoulders. I will make you get me water every time after - because I can, because you will.

It will be the relentless, unbearably alive kind of love that consumes you and me and everything in our vicinity. We will kill each other before we break apart, take cheap lovers when we want to breathe, and come back when breathing feels suffocating.

We will live extraordinary lives and write extraordinary prose, changing the world a word at a time. We will be each other's best audience and worst critic and hold the only opinions that matter. We will have enough love to give that it builds everyone around us, give them things we never got but have since healed in us.

I will be home to you and you to me - a house built on fire. And our works will outlast our bodies.

So what do you want from me? Because this is what I want and I won't settle for anything else. Keep your subpar alternatives to yourself. When what we can be is glorious, I don't want anything less.
